# Privacy notice — MVP draft

Driver Ledger AU stores ledger records, settings, declarations and receipt
images within the application's local storage on the device. The MVP does not
operate a cloud account or upload data to TenantSage infrastructure.

The device operating system, device backups and any files the user exports may
copy this information outside the app sandbox. Users should secure their device
and protect exported evidence packs.

Direct Uber/DiDi connections and ATO transmission are disabled. A future cloud
or provider integration requires a revised privacy notice, consent flow,
retention schedule, deletion mechanism, breach-response process and assessment
under the Australian Privacy Act and applicable provider terms before release.

This document is a product draft and must be completed with the operator's
legal entity, contact details, hosting locations and public privacy URL before
store publication.
